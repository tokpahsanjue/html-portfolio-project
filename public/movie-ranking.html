My Top 3 Movies

Into the badlands

Into the Badlands is a dark fantasy drama series that follows the story of a young woman named Ayla who is forced to navigate the treacherous world of the Badlands.

Game of Thrones

Game of Thrones is a fantasy drama series that follows the story of several noble families as they vie for control of the Iron Throne and the Seven Kingdoms of Westeros.

Breaking Bad

Breaking Bad is a crime drama series that follows the story of a high school teacher who becomes involved in the drug trade.
